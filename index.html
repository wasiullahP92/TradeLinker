Transparent Workflow

How TradeLinker Works

Experience a straightforward, hassle-free hiring process from discovery to service completion.

1

1. Find & Compare

Search by trade category, filter by your city or neighborhood, and review verified credentials, ratings, and past job histories.

2

2. Request Booking

Describe your problem, select your preferred date/time slot, and submit. The service provider receives an instant notification to review and accept.

3

3. Job Done & Review

The professional arrives and executes the work. Once marked completed, submit a verified rating to help your local community.

The TradeLinker Advantage

Built on Trust, Verification & Reliability

Traditional service hiring relies on word-of-mouth with no accountability. TradeLinker introduces modern transparency, document verification, and recorded booking lifecycles.

Admin-Controlled Verification (BR-07)

Trade certifications, CNIC identity, and experience checks administered by platform operators.

Full Booking Lifecycle Tracking (FR-33)

Real-time status updates: Pending, Accepted, In Progress, Completed, or Cancelled.

Strict Review Eligibility (BR-06)

Only customers with verified completed bookings can leave ratings, preventing spam or fake feedback.
